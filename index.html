How the monetary model works

1. Earn

Only verified, eligible bandwidth with attributable customer revenue creates a reward payable.

2. Back

Cash reserve is funded and reconciled before equivalent DTX is issued into circulation.

3. Spend

Users can transfer DTX or use it to purchase available data bundles and listed products.

4. Repurchase

Users return DTX for the published cash value, subject to available reserve, fees and payout checks.
